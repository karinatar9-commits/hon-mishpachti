# מי שילם? — Treatment (Isometric People Scene, 1080×1920, 30 fps, Hebrew, Karin's voice)

## Three candidate structures
1. **Mystery → solved (chosen).** One payment lands with no name; the owner hunts for it; then the same moment replayed with the agent: a lit path runs from the tray to the right invoice. A whodunit shape gives the script a hook ("ממי?") and a payoff ("שולם").
2. **Day in one shop as five small rules** (payment in, invoice out, reminder, approval, report). Clear but flat — five equal beats, no turn.
3. **Before / after rewind.** The owner's search runs, rewinds with a counter, replays with the agent. Strong native move, but doubles the length past 30 s.
Chosen: 1, borrowing the "rewind-less replay" idea from 3 only as a light-path reveal, to stay ~30 s for Instagram.

## Logline and arc
A ₪1,850 payment drops into a small studio's tray with no name; the owner tears the place apart looking for who paid — until a lit path finds the open invoice by itself and stamps it paid.
Setup: coin lands, "ממי?" → Turn: month end, accountant walks in, owner runs between phone / laptop / notes → Ending: the agent path finds the invoice, "שולם"; no match → a message waits on the owner's desk for approval; CTA.

## Benchmark
Isometric public-manners explainers and picture-book cutaways. Learn: calm iso staging, words lying on the floor, order of movement as the explanation. Don't take: characters, layouts, typefaces, music.

## Place
One small studio, cut-away (near walls low): street door bottom-left, an inbox tray on a counter by the door, the owner's desk centre, a shelf of open invoices top-right, the accountant's chair. Three stations for the search: a phone stand, a laptop table, a pin board of notes.

## Beat sheet / narration (one line per beat, starting with its action)
1 נכנסו לכם אלף שמונה מאות וחמישים שקל בביט. · 2 יופי! רק שאלה קטנה: ממי? · 3 אין שם, אין חשבונית, ואין לכם מושג על מה. · 4 בסוף החודש רואה החשבון שואל, ואתם מחפשים בוואטסאפ, במייל ובפתקים. · 5 סוכן בינה מלאכותית תופס כל תשלום ברגע שהוא נכנס, · 6 מוצא את החשבונית הפתוחה, ומסמן: שולם. · 7 ואם אין התאמה, הוא מכין ללקוח הודעה, ושולח רק אחרי שאישרתם. · 8 שלחו את זה לבעל עסק שעדיין מחפש מי שילם.

## Cue map
~100 bpm feel, no fixed bar lock (narration-driven). Vibraphone note per landing floor character; glock doubles accents (₪1,850, שולם). Pizzicato ticks during the search (beat 4) accelerate; a near-silence (0.4 s) right before the path starts (beat 5); first sound after it = a bright chime as the path starts.
